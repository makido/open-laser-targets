Shared page layout

The common frame is 210 × 279.4 mm. It is centred on A4 with an 8.8 mm top offset, or on Letter with a 2.95 mm left offset. Print at actual size / 100% with centring enabled. The four rifle editions place all three protected markers and the QR within a 126 × 108 mm rectangle. They preserve the original 25 mm outer scoring circle at 7.5 m.

Scoring and visible geometry

Swiss scoring zones are unchanged from the earlier definitions. Their symbol masks now follow the version 0.5 positions. All protected rectangles, including their boundaries, score zero. The 7.5 m ORD25 QR masks the lower-right part of the black artwork and a portion of the outer six-zone. Its 135 × 228 mm black rectangle is not rescaled. The 5 m black rectangle remains 90 × 152 mm. Both use boxy, square-ended numerals and the previously doubled stadium strokes. The short cubic approximation of the clipped six-zone remains unchanged.

The two Swiss B silhouettes and Morgarten preserve the bitmap-derived training traces, not independently verified production-master contours. Their sources and original measurements are in trace provenance and the Swiss dimension/source catalogue. Nominal circular and stadium scoring dimensions remain analytic.

NRA additions

The B-6 and B-8 editions follow target dimensions in rules 4.7–4.8 and the National Match Course mapping in the NRA Precision Pistol Rules, February 2026. Both are scaled for 5 yd; reference distances are 50 yd and 25 yd respectively. The black extends through the 8 ring on B-6 and 9 ring on B-8. Ring diameters and conversions are recorded in NRA dimension data. X rings are visible but score 10; OLTS has no separate X-count field. These are newly typeset practice reproductions, without an official competition seal.

Files and limits

SVGs contain vector artwork and symbols. PDF artwork is approximately 600 dpi, with vector QR and ArUco symbols. All 30 final A4 pages were scanned for QR and marker identity; all 15 target pages also passed the corresponding Letter-placement check. The checks cover geometry/control-point bounds, units, protection placement, QR capacity, scoring colors, distinct identities, and malformed layout rejection. Full build validation. Physical printer and app tests remain separate.

Swiss strips measure 50 mm on both axes; NRA strips measure 2 in (50.8 mm). Measure tick centres. The QR links use doxyo.com and contain the complete definition for offline decoding. No website landing service or app update is included in this build.
